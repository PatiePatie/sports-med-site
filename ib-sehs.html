Exam map

What the exam actually rewards

SEHS is a concept-based experimental science. Marks come from accurate content, application to unfamiliar data, and justified scientific reasoning—not isolated memorisation.

3

Integrated themes

Exercise physiology and nutrition · biomechanics · sports psychology and motor learning.

76%

External examination

Paper 1 combines multiple-choice and data questions. Paper 2 uses short and extended responses in context.

24%

Scientific investigation

One individual report, maximum 3,200 words, assessed on research design, data analysis, conclusion and evaluation.

Do not overstudy
If a fact is not needed to explain a syllabus concept, compare systems, interpret data, or justify an applied answer, it is probably not an exam priority.

Internal assessment

The scientific investigation: 24% in four equal criteria

Conclusion and evaluation together carry 50% of the IA. The mark comes from justification, not from a long methods section.

6

Research design

Specific contextual question, relevant data, reproducible method, controlled variables, safety and ethics.

6

Data analysis

Relevant, accurate and clear processing with appropriate uncertainty and correct units, graphs and tables.

6

Conclusion

A justified answer consistent with the analysis, scientific context and real-world implications.

6

Evaluation

Specific weaknesses, their relative impact and realistic improvements tied to those weaknesses.

Efficient study plan

Four passes, not endless rereading

Each pass has a different job. Stop when you can explain, compare and apply without notes.

1

Map

Read all 29 topic titles. Mark unknown concepts and HL-only material.

2

Learn

Use one topic at a time: concept → comparison → application → check.

3

Link

Connect physiology, biomechanics and psychology to the same performance problem.

4

Retrieve

Use past-paper command terms, data questions and timed writing.
